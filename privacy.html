Campfire Log Privacy policy

Your journal stays yours

Campfire Log has no accounts and no database of its own. Your playtime and notes live on your iPhone or iPad and in your own iCloud. This page explains the few things that do leave your device, where they go and why.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · applies to the Campfire Log app and campfirelog.eu

The short version

  • Your journal (playtime, notes, lists, medals) is stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud. I can't see it.
  • Game accounts you connect are used only to read your playtime from Steam, PlayStation and, later, Nintendo and Xbox. Sign-ins stay in your device's Keychain.
  • A small helper service looks up game info and Steam playtime for you. It passes requests along and keeps nothing.
  • Banner ads from Google appear on two screens. They're non-personalised, and a one-time purchase removes them.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no account. Delete my data in Settings wipes everything.

Who is responsible

Campfire Log is made by Robin van 't Slot, an independent developer in the Netherlands. For anything about your privacy, email hello@campfirelog.eu.

In the app

Your journal

Everything you log stays in the app's storage on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, the app syncs it between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. That copy is protected by your Apple Account and counts toward your own iCloud storage. I have no access to it. If you're not signed in to iCloud, the journal stays on that one device.

The app only reads. It never posts, buys, messages or changes anything on your game accounts. Sign-in tokens are sent only to the platform they belong to. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its token from your device.

The helper service

Covers, genres, time-to-beat and other game details come from IGDB, a game database run by Twitch. To keep secret keys out of the app, these lookups and Steam playtime requests pass through a small helper service that I run on Cloudflare. The helper receives what's needed to answer the request: the search words or game id, your SteamID for Steam playtime, and your IP address because that's how the internet works. It forwards the request to IGDB or Steam, returns the answer and stores nothing. Cloudflare runs the helper on my behalf and may keep short-lived technical logs for security.

Ads

Banner ads from Google AdMob appear on the History and Lists screens only. Ads are non-personalised, so they're not based on your interests or on other apps you use, and the app never asks to track you. To show an ad, Google still receives technical data such as your IP address, device type, operating system and an advertising-related identifier, and uses it to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud. If you're in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before the first ad. You can change that choice in Settings. Read how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Buy Remove Ads once and the ad service isn't started again on your devices.

Purchases

Purchases go through Apple. I never see your name or payment details. Apple only tells the app that you own Remove Ads.

Reminders and widgets

The evening reminder is scheduled on your device. Widgets read the same on-device journal. No reminder or widget data is sent anywhere.

Crash reports

If you've allowed your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple sends me anonymous crash reports. You can turn this off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. The app has no analytics of its own.

On campfirelog.eu and the waitlist

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads its fonts from its own server. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you join the waitlist, your email address and the platforms you picked are stored with Kit, the email service that sends Campfire Log's emails. You confirm your address first, and they're used only to tell you about Campfire Log's beta and launch. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing or asking me removes you. See Kit's privacy policy.

If you email me, the message is kept in my iCloud Mail so I can reply.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings > Delete my data removes your journal from this device and your iCloud, and signs out of every game account. Deleting the app removes the local copy too.
  • Disconnect an account at any time in the Accounts tab.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data I hold, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Email hello@campfirelog.eu and I'll reply within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you can contact the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority where you live.

Children

Campfire Log is not made for children under 16, and they shouldn't use it without a parent. When a parent connects Nintendo Parental Controls, the app reads their child's Switch playtime into the parent's own journal on the parent's device. It goes nowhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your device, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
